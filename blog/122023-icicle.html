My ICICLE Education Fellow Experience

A few thoughts from the fellowship.

Academic PhD Fellowship

TLDR: Really enjoyed my time with the ICICLE Education Fellows program.

For a few months, I have had the pleasure of serving as an Educational Fellow with the ICICLE program. Creating a proposal, deciding on the scope of the work and finally managing all the different aspects of the project has been a great opportunity for growth.

Last month, I presented on my project at the All Hands Meeting to all the faculty, administrators, partners, and other fellows. The final report for my work is available to read here.

From the ICICLE website, “Led by The Ohio State University, the U.S. National Science Foundation funded AI institute for Intelligent Cyberinfrastructure with Computational Learning in the Environment (ICICLE) will build the next generation of Cyberinfrastructure to render Artificial Intelligence (AI) more accessible to everyone and drive its further democratization in the larger society.”
